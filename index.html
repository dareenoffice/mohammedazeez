English

Muhammad Azeez is specializing in the import and wholesale distribution of PPF (Paint Protection Film), Vinyl Wrap, Window Film, Reflective Sheeting, and professional installation tools and materials. Our headquarters are located in Erbil, Iraq. We import our products directly from leading manufacturers in China and trusted companies in the United Arab Emirates (UAE), then distribute them wholesale to markets and dealers across all provinces of Iraq.

العربية
